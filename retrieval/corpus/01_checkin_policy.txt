Check-in Policy

Grand Horizon Hotel & Suites

Standard check-in time is 2:00 PM. Guests are required to present a valid government-issued photo ID and the booking confirmation at the reception. A refundable security deposit may be collected during check-in, depending on the room category. Early check-in is subject to room availability and hotel policies. Guests arriving before the standard check-in time may request luggage storage free of charge while waiting for their room.
