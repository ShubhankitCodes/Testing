Late Check-out Policy

Grand Horizon Hotel & Suites

Late check-out is available upon request and depends on room availability. Guests checking out between 11:00 AM and 2:00 PM may be charged a partial room rate. Check-outs after 2:00 PM are generally charged as a full night's stay. Guests are advised to contact the Front Desk at least one day in advance to request a late check-out.
