Breakfast Timings

Grand Horizon Hotel & Suites

Complimentary breakfast is served daily from 7:00 AM to 10:30 AM at the Horizon Restaurant on the ground floor. The buffet includes Indian, Continental, and healthy breakfast options along with fresh juices, tea, and coffee. Guests are requested to carry their room key or breakfast coupon while entering the restaurant. Breakfast service concludes promptly at 10:30 AM.
