Deluxe Room Information

Grand Horizon Hotel & Suites

The Deluxe Room offers a comfortable stay with a king-size bed, air conditioning, a smart TV, complimentary Wi-Fi, tea and coffee-making facilities, and a work desk. Each room includes an attached bathroom with premium toiletries and a hairdryer. The room accommodates up to two adults and one child. Daily housekeeping is included in the room rate.
